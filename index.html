01 • MSTC

What is MST Blockchain?

MST Blockchain is presented by its official materials as a public, EVM-compatible Layer-1 blockchain developed by Masterstroke Technosoft.

Layer 1

MST operates as its own blockchain network rather than simply being a token deployed on another blockchain.

EVM Compatible

Developers can use Ethereum-compatible development tools and Solidity-based smart contracts.

MSTC

MSTC is the native blockchain asset referred to throughout the MST ecosystem and network materials.

Public Network

The official MST website describes MST as a public Layer-1 network whose blockchain data can be publicly verified.

Validators

Validators participate in transaction validation and network consensus according to the MST protocol.

Smart Contracts

MST supports smart-contract development using Solidity and familiar Ethereum development tools.

06 • Validators

How Validator Participation Works

Validators are an important part of blockchain consensus. MST provides both technical validator participation and fractional participation options through its portal.

Network participation

A validator participates in validating transactions and supporting the network's consensus process.

Full validator

MST's official technical material says a full validator requires MSTC staking and the required node infrastructure.

Fractional validator

MST also provides a fractional validator option that is designed to avoid the need for users to operate their own technical server infrastructure.

Protocol rewards

Validator rewards, when applicable, are determined by protocol rules and are not guaranteed.


Important: Validator participation should not be treated as guaranteed income. MST's own portal states that rewards are protocol determined and not guaranteed, and that participation is not an investment, deposit or financial product.

12 • FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is MSTC?

MSTC is the native asset associated with the MST Blockchain ecosystem.

Is MST a Layer-1 blockchain?

Yes. MST's official materials describe MST Blockchain as a public Layer-1 blockchain.

Is MST EVM compatible?

Yes. MST's official technical material states that MST is EVM compatible and supports Solidity and familiar Ethereum development tools.

Can I build an NFT on MST?

Yes. EVM-compatible smart contracts can be used to develop NFT applications, subject to the current MST network and tooling configuration.

Can I build a DeFi application?

MST's ecosystem materials identify DeFi as one of the application categories supported by its infrastructure.

Can I become a validator?

MST provides validator participation options. Full validator operation requires the infrastructure and staking described by MST, while fractional validator participation is also offered through the MST portal.

Are validator rewards guaranteed?

No. MST's official portal explicitly states that applicable validator rewards are protocol-determined and not guaranteed.

Is MSTC legal tender in India?

This website does not describe MSTC as legal tender in India. Blockchain operation, company registration, token use and legal-tender status are separate concepts.

Can businesses build on MST?

Yes. MST's published ecosystem materials identify enterprise, supply-chain, real-estate, healthcare, tokenization and other application categories.

Where can I verify MST transactions?

Transactions and public blockchain information can be examined through MSTScan.
